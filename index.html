Elina M. Dilger

I am a political economist working on climate policy. I study why some climate policies last while others are weakened or reversed.

About

Alongside that, I work on climate finance, energy markets and green central banking, and I am increasingly interested in AI safety and what AI means for energy and climate policy.

I am a PhD candidate at the London School of Economics and Political Science, where I teach seminars on climate governance and environmental economics. I also work on policy, most recently on intergenerationally fair budgeting for Germany's Federal Ministry for Family Affairs.

Outside work, I hike, climb, cycle and run, and at home I cook and knit.
