Elina M. Dilger

I am a political economist working on climate and energy policy.

Currently, I am pursuing a PhD in Environmental Economics at the London School of Economics and Political Science and the Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change and the Environment under the supervision of Marion Dumas and Richard Perkins.

About

My research focuses on the interaction of economic climate policy and politics. Specifically, I am interested in how political institutions shape the green transition and economic and political realities.

My current research projects examine:

  • the durability and post-adoption trajectories of climate policies
  • the effect of climate policies and damages on populism
  • the politicisation of central banks on the issue of climate policy and its effect on financial markets
  • green fiscal forward guidance
  • state-owned investment banks and their role in the green transition
  • data centres and energy markets

Besides this, I am increasingly interested in AI safety and the effects of AI on energy and labour markets.

Outside of academia, I contribute to policy work, most recently on intergenerationally fair budgeting for Germany's Federal Ministry for Family Affairs and FiscalFuture.
